What I Built
🗄️ Designed a full ERD and normalised the schema from 1NF through to 3NF, eliminating all partial and transitive dependencies across 6 tables: Applicant, Job, Interview, Interviewer, Documents, and Job Offer.
⚡ Built an automatic trigger (UpdateJobOfferStatusNotification) that fires after any job offer status change and inserts a notification record into the Documents table for the relevant applicant.
📦 Created stored procedures including GetJobOffersForApplicant and GetJobsByLocation to encapsulate common queries for HR teams.
🔢 Implemented custom SQL functions — CountJobOffersForJob and CalculateAverageSalary — to support business analytics on hiring activity.
📋 Wrote a full data dictionary covering all attribute names, data types, formats, and primary/foreign key relationships across every table.
💼 Delivered a business analysis section covering real-world problem alignment, SWOT analysis, potential users, and implementation challenges.
